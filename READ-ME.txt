CEYLON FLEA MARKET — INTERACTIVE DEMO

1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge or another modern browser.
3. The site starts on Market Days. Click the Ceylon logo to see the homepage, or use the menu to explore.

This is a standalone demonstration, not the live server. Test accounts, uploaded photos and other changes disappear when you reload the page. There is no live Facebook sync or payment processing.

To try the organiser tools, choose “Organiser: add photos” and enter the demo password shown on the sign-in page.
