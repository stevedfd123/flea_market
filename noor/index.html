For Noor, and what comes next

Hi Noor, opening a natural wine and ceramics bar feels like the most natural next step for you. I put this page together because the way you set a table, glaze a cup and host a room already reads like a brand. It deserves a home that feels the same.

My name is Ines. Together with my partner Theo, I run a small studio called ostra. We make brands, websites and digital products for founder-led places. Most of these turn into years-long relationships, long after the doors first open.

A bar like yours only works when it
feels like a place, not a product.

Anything less feels borrowed. We stay close to the craft, work side by side with founders and skip the templates, so what we make feels as handmade as the cups you throw. The work lasts because it starts from you.

The projects below each show a different part of what a place like yours needs: warm, tactile storytelling for people who come back, not a shop that looks like it was set up in an afternoon.

Thank you for reading this far, Noor. We'd love to hear from you, zero commitment.

Ines
